## Method

## Observations

<Aside type="note" title="Observed">
  Record direct observations, their order and test conditions. Label user

</Aside>

## Interpretation

Explain how the observations support or weaken each explanation. A successful workaround does not by itself establish a root cause.

<Aside type="note" title="Inferred">
  State the inference and the evidence connecting it to the observations.
  Include credible alternative explanations that remain possible.
</Aside>

## Conclusion and limits

Answer the original question as narrowly as the evidence allows. State what changed, what was verified and what remains uncertain.

<Aside type="note" title="Not verified">
  Record outstanding checks and the limits of the conclusion. Do not turn an
  untested possibility into a statement of fact.
</Aside>
